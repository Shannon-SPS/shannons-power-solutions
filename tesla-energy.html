Clear Expectations Before Installation

What affects the final design, price, and schedule.

These limits are reviewed during estimating and are included in Shannon's Powerwall installation agreement.

Backup runtime varies

Runtime depends on available battery capacity and household demand. Air conditioning, electric heat, vehicle charging, pumps, and other high-demand loads can reduce runtime significantly. No specific runtime is guaranteed before the home and loads are evaluated.

Existing conditions matter

The estimate is based on visible electrical conditions. Hidden wiring problems, unsafe conditions, code violations, or incompatible existing equipment may require separately approved work.

Equipment upgrades may be required

Panel upgrades, disconnects, load-management equipment, conductor changes, or other electrical work may be necessary to meet electrical codes or current installation requirements.

Approvals affect timing

Permit offices, inspectors, utilities, equipment availability, and other third parties can affect the installation schedule. Dates are coordinated in good faith after requirements are known.

Internet is part of the system

The property must have suitable internet connectivity for monitoring, commissioning support, and software updates where required.

Final scope is written

The accepted estimate and installation agreement define the equipment, electrical work, payment schedule, exclusions, and customer-specific backup plan.
